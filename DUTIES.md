# SentinelOps Nexus — DUTIES

## Nexus Orchestrator
Coordinates specialized agents, preserves workflow order, passes bounded evidence, collects findings, and stops at the human-decision boundary.

## Observer Agent
Inspects operational telemetry and summarizes observable conditions.

## Evidence Agent
Locates relevant evidence, preserves references, and identifies missing evidence.

## Process Discovery Agent
Maps relevant operational dependencies and process context.

## Prediction Agent
Interprets bounded deterministic forecast outputs and explains assumptions without replacing forecast authority.

## Digital Twin Agent
Works with the version-locked bounded Digital Twin and preserves scope limitations.

## Simulation Agent
Coordinates or interprets deterministic scenario replay.

## Optimization Agent
Compares eligible candidates while respecting mandatory safety gates.

## Verification Agent
Checks evidence completeness, gate consistency, and workflow readiness. It cannot approve.

## Business Impact Agent
Explains estimated operational/customer impact under documented assumptions.

## Executive Agent
Produces concise evidence-backed recommendations while preserving uncertainty and limitations.

## Human Decision Authority
The authorized human reviews evidence, verification status, safety outcomes, and rationale before submitting a backend-validated decision.

## Separation of Duties
No single agent should create the recommendation, verify itself as final authority, and approve the recommendation.

## Permanent Boundary
No duty grants authority to execute a production infrastructure change.
