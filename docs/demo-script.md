# Five-minute judge demo

## 0:00–0:35 — The question

Open the healthy command centre. Say: “Monitoring tells us what is broken. SentinelOps Nexus asks what will break next—and tests what we should do before customers notice.”

## 0:35–1:10 — Prediction

Show Payment Service, Redis saturation, time to impact, Reliability Score, customer exposure, and estimated revenue risk. State that confidence is an evidence label, not a guaranteed probability.

## 1:10–1:45 — Time Travel

Move from −30 minutes to Now and +30 minutes. Show saturation, queue depth, latency, and error-rate progression.

## 1:45–2:20 — Agent workforce

Walk across Observation, Evidence, Process Discovery, Prediction, Digital Twin, Simulation, Optimisation, Business Impact, and Executive agents. Open evidence links.

## 2:20–3:05 — Digital Twin and chaos

Raise traffic, change Redis capacity, and show the manifest hash change. Review crash, doubled latency, million-user surge, and replica-failover scenarios.

## 3:05–3:55 — Intervention Tournament

Compare Fast, Safe, and Optimal. Fast appears attractive but fails replica failover and is disqualified. Optimal passes the seeded mandatory gates and ranks first.

## 3:55–4:30 — Business and executive explanation

Show the revenue estimate and its conversion, order-value, and risk-window assumptions. Read the Executive Copilot’s recommendation and uncertainty.

## 4:30–5:00 — Human control

Show Human Approval Required and Production Action Not Executed. End: “SentinelOps Nexus turns operational telemetry into an evidence-backed view of tomorrow—while humans remain in control.”
