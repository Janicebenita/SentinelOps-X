## Verified public rules

- The challenge is organized by **HiDevs × Lyzr**, presented by AI House.
- The public schedule states a 21-day challenge running **2026-09-13 through 2026-10-03**. The Unstop registration deadline was **2026-10-03 10:30 PM IST** and is marked closed.
- Public contribution paths are:
  - Agent Building
  - Framework Migration
  - Open Source Contributions
  - Technical Deep Dives
- Public descriptions emphasize modular, portable, verifiable agent systems and cross-framework interoperability.
- Final submissions must include source code, architecture details, documentation, verification evidence, and the required technical checkpoints.
- Public evaluation criteria include architecture and modularity, portability, framework interoperability, identity and behavior contracts, tool design, implementation, verification results, documentation, innovation, and practical impact.
- Submissions must be original work by the participant or team. Third-party components must be used under their licenses and major third-party components should be disclosed.
- Only submissions through the designated HiDevs challenge platform count for official scoring.
- Plagiarism, fraudulent verification, manipulated results, or misrepresentation can lead to disqualification.
- The public prize pool is **USD 500**. Standout or winning submissions may receive 30-day placement in the GitAgent Registry.

## Eligibility questions that remain unresolved

The following require the authenticated platform rules or organizer confirmation:

1. Whether submissions or updates are still accepted after 2026-10-03.
2. Whether a validated submission can be updated in place or resubmitted.
3. Whether previously existing repositories are eligible without a minimum in-window commit threshold.
4. Whether one repository may be used for multiple tracks.
5. Whether multiple submissions in one track are allowed.
6. How the platform treats substantially overlapping repositories or duplicate agents.
7. Whether a fork-based upstream contribution is eligible before merge, and what proof of merge is required.
8. Whether OpenGAP is mandatory. No official OpenGAP requirement or specification was found in the public sources reviewed.
9. Which target frameworks qualify for the migration track and whether a specific target is mandated.
10. Exact scoring events, caps, tie-breaking, judging dates, and award-to-points relationships.
11. Restrictions on AI-assisted implementation beyond the general originality and misrepresentation rules.

## Current compliance decision

- Do **not** claim new points, awards, or rank changes from repository improvements alone.
- Do **not** implement a purported OpenGAP protocol without an official specification.
- Do **not** resubmit overlapping SentinelOps-X and Bottleneck-IQ work until the duplicate-submission rule is known.
- Because the public challenge window is closed, all new work is **submission-ready portfolio work**, not an assumed scorable submission.
- Obtain organizer confirmation before any new submission, resubmission, publication, or public award claim.
