# Limitations

- The public finale uses deterministic seeded telemetry, not a live enterprise estate.
- The capacity projection is transparent and reproducible but not a general forecasting benchmark.
- Confidence is a heuristic evidence score and is not calibrated probability.
- Business impact depends on displayed assumptions and is not an accounting result.
- Causality is not guaranteed; the graph expresses evidence and assumptions.
- The current demo does not execute Gemini, ADK, MCP, A2A, Vertex AI, Kubernetes, or production observability connectors unless separately configured and demonstrated.
- No intervention or deployment path executes automatically.
